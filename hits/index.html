Where the Hits Land

Every hit of the 2025-26 NHL regular season, drawn from the hitter's side so each team attacks left to right. Zoom, filter, and hover.

Hover the rink to count hits within 10 ft (3 m) of that spot (circle area 314 sq ft, 29 m²), next to the rink-wide average per 100 sq ft. Hover the bars outside the boards for hits per foot of wall. Bars count hits within 6 ft (1.8 m) of the boards, in 2 ft (0.6 m) slices of wall, and are scaled to the current selection.

Method: every regular-season game from the NHL's public play-by-play feed, keeping events logged as hits. Each hit is flipped so the hitter attacks to the right, and points are nudged up to half a foot so they don't stack on a grid. The glow is hit density smoothed over about 2.4 ft (0.7 m). Positions are where the NHL logged the hit, not who hit whom; hits are credited to the hitter's team. Rink: 200 × 85 ft (61 × 25.9 m).

Data: NHL.com play-by-play feed, 2025-26 regular season (1,312 games). Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NHL. Map by Ridgeline Maps, Edwards, Colorado. All hockey maps
